Welcome to SLURP! Every major interaction on this page is wired with Alpine.js — let's click through the highlights.

First up: the hero dare. Tap the catch button and Chef Marco throws a full SVG splash at your screen — smears, strands, garnishes, the works.

Pure CSS and SVG keyframes, with JavaScript seeding random extra noodle strands on each replay. Close the modal when you're done getting roasted.

Back to the hero. Notice the frosted desktop nav — pill-shaped links with orange active states and a scroll-progress bar up top.

Click a nav pill and the page scrolls to that section. The active link updates as you move — scroll spy baked into one Alpine component.

Long lists use peacock show-more buttons instead of pagination. Three bowls show first — click the peacock to fan out the rest of the vault.

Build Your Bowl is CSS-only: broth color, noodle style, and toppings all update a live plate preview via custom properties — no image swaps.

Pick your broth, noodle style, and toppings. Each chip toggles with aria-pressed so screen readers know what's selected.

Lock in your plate, then choose Buy or Learn. Buy drops a generated bowl name straight into the cart.

Added to cart — the floating checkout bar and header badge bump to match.

Gift a Bowl uses searchable CollectUI-style pickers for delivery location and preset messages — both wired with scoped click-outside handlers.

Open the location picker, search a city or store, and the live gift card preview updates as you type.

Same pattern for preset messages — pick a note and watch it land on the gift card preview.

Slurp Support: contact channel cards plus an FAQ accordion. Click a question and the answer expands in place.

Four FAQs show first; the peacock reveals the rest — same progressive-disclosure pattern as menu and reviews.

Finally, checkout — your cart summary, qty steppers, and a client-side order flow. Every button click you just saw lives in one index.html file.
