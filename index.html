Earth Is Too Small For The Love Of Noodles

Humans slurped every bowl. Cities ran out of broth. Continents ran out of chopsticks. The noodle love became too powerful for one planet.

Three thousand years from the first hand-pulled strand — the broth is still steaming, still in the middle, still worth crossing a galaxy for.

Even the aliens heard the slurp echo across the galaxy — and they love it too. Now they're leaving Earth, spinning off into space with bowls in hand, searching for the next planet worthy of a proper SLURP!

Lời chào · The greeting

Slurped yet?

"Have you slurped yet?"

It is how we say hello on the phone, to a neighbour, to someone walking through the door with empty hands and hopeful eyes. It also means I was thinking about you, and come inside, and there is still broth in the pot.

There is no way to translate it that does not come out sounding like hunger — which, honestly, is the point.
